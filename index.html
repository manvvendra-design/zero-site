The mark. Zero is drawn by hand. The Z is a snake coming down from the sky; the O is a tree putting down roots. That's where this site's address comes from.

What is Zero?

A universe set in the Dibang Valley of Arunachal Pradesh, where the Mishmi hills meet the clouds. It begins with The One True King, the old folk tale the whole valley grows from, and goes on to Arun and Emuli. The same world will grow into vertical dramas, a series, a game and a film.
