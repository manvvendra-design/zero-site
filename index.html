The mark. Zero is drawn by hand. The Z is a snake coming down from the sky; the O is a tree putting down roots. That's where this site's address comes from.

What is Zero?

A universe set in the Dibang Valley of Arunachal Pradesh, where the Mishmi hills meet the clouds. Book One follows Arun and Emuli through romance, adventure and old folklore. The same world will grow into vertical dramas, a series, a game and a film.
