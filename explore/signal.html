One light-day

18 Nov 2026 · Voyager 1 · NASA

On 18 November 2026 Voyager 1 became the first spacecraft ever to be one light-day from Earth: so far away that a radio message, moving at the speed of light, takes a whole day to reach it. It launched in 1977.

Send it a message. Your signal leaves Earth at the speed of light. Speed up time to follow it past the Moon, the planets and the edge of the Sun’s bubble, and see how long the journey really is.
